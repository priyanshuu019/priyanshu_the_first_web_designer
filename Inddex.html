Love / Pyar

"Teraa sath hai toh mujhe kya chahiye,
Meri har khushi ka tu hi sahara hai."

Sad / Dard

"Khamoshi se pyaar karna seekh liya humne,
Ab kisi ke jaane ka dard nahi hota."

Friendship / Dosti

"Sacha dost wohi hai jo ansu bhi pehchan le,
Jabki baaki sab hasi dekh kar khush ho jayein."
